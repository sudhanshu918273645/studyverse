StudyVerse Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

1. Introduction

StudyVerse is a learning application designed to help students organize their study activities and learning content. This policy explains how the application handles information when you use its features.

2. Information We Handle

Depending on the features you use, StudyVerse may handle account information used for authentication, content you create, files you choose to upload or manage through Google Drive, and technical information needed to operate and secure the application.

3. Google Sign-In

StudyVerse may use Google Sign-In through Supabase Authentication. Signing in to StudyVerse and connecting Google Drive are separate actions.

4. Google Drive Access

If you choose to connect Google Drive, StudyVerse requests the permissions shown on Google's consent screen to provide the Drive features you use. Your Drive connection is optional. You can revoke access through your Google Account settings. Files already created in Google Drive may remain there after access is revoked.

5. Storage and Service Providers

StudyVerse uses services such as Supabase for authentication and configured backend features, and Google Drive for files you choose to store there.

6. Sharing and Visibility

Content visibility depends on the feature and its configuration. Do not upload confidential or sensitive information to files or folders configured for public link access. Anyone with access to a public link may be able to view that content.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Some information may remain in Google Drive or other services after you disconnect your account. You may need to delete files separately from the service where they are stored.

8. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. Never share your passwords or authentication codes.

9. Children's Privacy

StudyVerse is intended for students. Where required by applicable law, minors should use the application with appropriate parent or guardian guidance.

10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as StudyVerse's features change. The latest version will be published on this page.
